Regulatory tracking

Rete

Tracks regulatory changes as laws move through the process, shows exactly what changed in the text and posts updates to Slack.

Rebuilt with made-up data. The real tool is under NDA.

How it works

Rete tracks regulation as it changes, shows the team exactly what changed in the law, and flags it the moment something moves.

Public registers→Extractor agent→Verifier agent→Change log→MapSlack appThis Week
  1. Every week a scheduled job looks for new texts in the public registers.
  2. An AI agent quotes each watched provision word for word; a second agent checks that quote against the source page. Both reply in JSON through an API.
  3. The app compares each quote with the last version and saves what changed.
  4. The map links members' public statements to the provisions they mention, so their positions move when a text moves.
  5. A Slack app posts what changed. Anything the verifier can't confirm goes to a lawyer's review queue instead.
