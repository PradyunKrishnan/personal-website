Brand protection

Copy Cat

Finds look-alike brands and copied wording, ranks the risk and sends each case to the right reviewer.

Rebuilt with made-up data. The real tool is under NDA.

How it works

Copy Cat looks for anyone copying a brand online and helps the legal team deal with each one.

Brand profile→Scan agent→Severity rules→Findings register→Review boardSlack alert
  1. The brand's marks, domains and official accounts sit in one profile, taken from the contract and a trademark list.
  2. Every Friday a scheduled job asks an AI agent to search the web, new certificates and domain records for anything that looks like the brand.
  3. Each hit is scored HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW and saved in a register, so nothing is reported twice.
  4. The team moves each finding from New to In review to Actioned, with notes.
  5. HIGH findings also go straight to Slack, so someone acts the same day.
